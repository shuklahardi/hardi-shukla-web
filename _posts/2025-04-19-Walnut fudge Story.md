This story takes place in the Bluehill Forest and involves a treasure hunt for special walnuts. For this, I wanted to create a rich, inviting background world. 
The Characters We have a squirrel, a mouse, and a parrot working together. I gave each of them a small accessory to make them unique. 

![My Image](/assets/images/walnutfudge/walnut%20fudge%201%20(1).webp)

![My Image](/assets/images/walnutfudge/walnut%20fudge%201%20(2).webp)

The Fair Atmosphere The final scene at the Spring Fair needed to look busy. I filled the background with stalls, banners, and other animals. Even though the main focus is on the fudge stall, adding these background details makes the world feel lived-in and real.
